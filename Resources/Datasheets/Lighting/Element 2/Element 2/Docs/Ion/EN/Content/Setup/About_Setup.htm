About Setup

This section describes the processes involved in changing your system, user, and device settings to meet your preferences.

Several of the options found in Setup can also be configured from the ECU. Their settings are shared between both areas. For more information, see Overview for more information.

To enter the setup screen, press [Displays]> {Setup} or go to the Browser>Setup.

The CIA will repaint to display the setup screen and the softkeys will change to display the various subcategories of setup. Ion defaults to display show settings, however if you have changed the view to another subcategory, Ion will remember the view you were in when you return to setup.

The setup subcategory softkeys are:

  • System
  • User
  • Device
