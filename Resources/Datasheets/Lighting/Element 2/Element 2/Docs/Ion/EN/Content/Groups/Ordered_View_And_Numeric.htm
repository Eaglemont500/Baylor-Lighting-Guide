Ordered View and Numeric View

By default, grouped channels will be displayed in ordered view. Therefore, grouped channels will appear in the order they were added to the group (see Ordered Channels ). If you wish to view the channels in numeric view, press the [Format] key and the view will be switched (channels will appear in numeric order from lowest to highest).

This setting is important in defining next/last functionality within groups in live/blind. If left in numeric format, when using [Next]/[Last] group selection channels will be selected in numeric order. If left in ordered view, they will be selected based on their order of being stored to the group.
