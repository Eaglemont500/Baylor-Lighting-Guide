Assigning Cue Attributes

You can record cues with specific attributes to affect how cues behave when executed. Cue attributes include Follow, Hang, Link, Loop, Delay Time, Rate, Curve, Cue Notes, Cue Labels, and Scenes. Cue attributes can be entered when the cue is initially recorded, or they can be added or modified at a later date.

When [Cue] is pressed, the {Attributes} softkey will display. Press {Attributes} to access the following softkeys:

  • {Rate}
  • {Note}
  • {Scene}
  • {Curve}
  • {Preheat}
  • {AF/MF} (Allfade/ Move Fade)
  • {Scene End}

Note: Preheat and Allfade are flags. Please see Preheat and AllFade for more information.
