Using Blackout and Grandmaster

A grandmaster inhibits all live intensity values. If a grandmaster is set at 50%, all live intensities will be at 50% of their actual values.

On a physical fader that is configured to be a grandmaster, pressing the fader’s [Go] & [Stop/Back] buttons at the same time will act as a blackout and inhibit all intensities to 0%. Pressing [Go] & [Stop/Back] again will exit blackout.

When a grandmaster or blackout are active, a grandmaster/ blackout warning will display at the top of all monitors. Clicking that will open a dialogue in the CIA.
