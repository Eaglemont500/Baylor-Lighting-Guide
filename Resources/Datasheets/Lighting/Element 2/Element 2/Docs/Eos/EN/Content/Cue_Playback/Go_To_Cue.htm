Go To Cue

[Go to Cue] instructions can be executed from any operating mode. By default, a [Go to Cue] instruction is an out-of-sequence cue and will follow the rules of such (see Out-of-Sequence Cues ).

Following are examples of [Go To Cue]:

  • [Go To Cue] [5] [Enter] - all parameters with values in cue 5 are faded to those values, even if they are tracked.
  • [Go to Cue] [6] [At] [5] [Enter] - takes you to cue 6 at 50% of its fade completion. Hitting [Go] would finish the cue.
  • [Go To Cue] [Next] [Enter] - takes you to the next cue in the active list.
  • [Go To Cue] [Last] [Enter] - takes you to the previous cue in the active list.

When a [Go To Cue] instruction is executed, any null states applied with {Make Null} are removed. To maintain the make null setting, you should use [Capture] for the required channels. For more information, see Using {Make Null} andUsing [Capture] .
